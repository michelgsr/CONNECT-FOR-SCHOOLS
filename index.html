How it works

Four steps for the tutor

About 20 minutes once a term. Students don’t need an app, an account or a phone to take part.

Less screen time, by design

Who does what

The tutor

Uses CONNECT for a few minutes: chooses activities near the school, prints the materials and forms groups.

Students

See the board in class, sign up on paper and go to the activity together. No app, no account.

Families

Get a letter from the school and register on the organiser’s official page when needed.

Organisers

Keep running their activities and registrations. CONNECT only points to their official page.

    For families

    The school brings the activities. Your child lives them.

    CONNECT is a tool for teachers. The tutor chooses free and low-cost activities near the school, puts them on a board in class and sends you a letter with the details.

    Your child doesn’t need an app or an account: they sign up on a paper sheet in class if they’re interested, and can go with classmates.

    Privacy

    What CONNECT does and doesn’t do

    Registration and payments

    Registration, permissions and payments (if any) are always handled by the official organiser of each activity. The letter from the school includes the official link for each one.

    First visit as a group

    The school may organise a first visit to an activity as a group, accompanied by a teacher or educator, so nobody has to go alone the first time. For this, the school will ask you to sign the authorisation at the bottom of the letter.

    About CONNECT

    Less scrolling. More neighbourhood.

    Young people spend a lot of time on screens and social media. Meanwhile, Barcelona has hundreds of free activities for them, but the information is scattered and many teens never hear about it.

    Our approach: schools as the bridge. Instead of another app for teenagers, CONNECT is a tool that helps tutors bring the activities that already exist in the neighbourhood to their class, on paper and in person.

    CONNECT does
    • Gather free and low-cost activities from official sources
    • Help tutors choose the ones near their school
    • Create printable materials for class and families
    • Help students go together, organised by the school
    CONNECT doesn’t
    • Create or run activities
    • Manage registrations or payments
    • Ask students to download an app or create an account
    • Work as a social network: no profiles, no chats
    Always free

    Free for students and schools

    No cost, no advertising, no sign-up.
